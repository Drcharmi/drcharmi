Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is dental treatment painful? +
Most treatments are painless. We use modern techniques and anesthesia to keep you comfortable.
Is teeth whitening safe for sensitive teeth?+
Yes! We use professional-grade, sensitivity-optimized whitening systems. For patients with high sensitivity, we recommend our gentle protocol with desensitizing pre-treatment, which minimizes discomfort significantly.
How many Aligners sessions are needed?+
The number of aligners (and sessions) varies per case. Mild corrections may require 6–12 months, while comprehensive treatment can take 12–24 months. Dr. Charmi will provide a precise timeline during your consultation.
What is the recovery time for dental implants?+
Initial healing takes 2–3 days. Osseointegration (bone fusion with the implant) takes 3–6 months. During this period, you can resume normal activities within 48 hours. The final crown placement completes the procedure.
What makes your clinic different from others?+
We combine clinical excellence with a luxury patient experience. From our 3D smile planning technology to our artisan-crafted restorations imported from the finest ceramists in Europe — every detail is curated for your perfect outcome.
